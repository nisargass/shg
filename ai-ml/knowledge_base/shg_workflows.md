# SHG Workflows and Governance Guidelines

## 1. Structure of Self-Help Groups (SHGs)
A Self-Help Group (SHG) is an affinity-based, democratic collective of 10 to 20 rural or semi-urban women. The group functions under the principles of mutual trust, shared accountability, and collective empowerment.

### Key Office Bearers
1. **President / Leader:** Oversees group meetings, liaises with village federations (Gram Panchayat Level Federations / VOs), coordinates bulk order contracts, and ensures democratic consensus.
2. **Secretary:** Maintains group registers, records meeting minutes, tracks attendance, and manages outward correspondence.
3. **Treasurer:** Manages the group's bank account, records member savings, manages loan repayments, and maintains financial transparency.
4. **Community Resource Person (CRP) / Animator:** Assists with digital data entry, bookkeeping, and technology tools like SHG Saathi.

## 2. Weekly Meeting Protocol (Panchasutra)
Every well-governed SHG strictly adheres to the **Panchasutra** (Five Core Principles):
1. **Regular Weekly Meetings:** Every member attends on fixed days.
2. **Regular Savings:** Fixed minimum contribution per member deposited into the common pool.
3. **Regular Internal Lending:** Urgent member credit needs met from the group's revolving fund.
4. **Timely Repayment:** Zero-default culture for internal micro-loans.
5. **Transparent Bookkeeping:** Up-to-date ledgers, passbooks, and digital records visible to all members.

## 3. Order Governance on SHG Saathi
When a commercial enterprise or government buyer places an order through the platform:
- The President reviews the delivery timeline and unit specifications.
- Work is distributed transparently across members based on demonstrated craft skills and verified daily capacity.
- No single member may be overburdened with quantities exceeding their feasible capacity.
