# Bulk Order Fulfillment and Production SOP

## 1. End-to-End Order Lifecycle
Order fulfillment follows a structured five-stage workflow to maintain institutional quality:

### Stage 1: Order Acceptance & Pricing Review
- The buyer submits an order request with clear technical specifications (dimensions, materials, color shades, tolerances).
- The SHG leadership runs the intelligent pricing calculator to ensure full recovery of material, artisan wages, packaging, and logistics overheads.
- The buyer approves the proposed quote and places a 30% advance deposit into the group's escrow/bank account.

### Stage 2: Raw Material Procurement & Reservation
- Required quantities of raw fabrics, threads, dyes, or inputs are verified in inventory.
- Inventory is formally tagged with `BULK_RESERVED` status. Under no circumstances may stock reserved for bulk orders be sold to walk-in retail customers.

### Stage 3: Fair Work Allocation
- Tasks (cutting, block printing, stitching, trimming) are allocated to members based on verified daily capacity and skill ratings.
- Deadlines are spaced with a minimum 2-day buffer prior to the final shipment target.

### Stage 4: Production Batches & Quality Inspection
- Work is completed in discrete production batches (e.g. 50 units per batch).
- Batch photographs are uploaded to SHG Saathi for automated visual quality assessment.
- Final acceptance or rework directives remain the sole responsibility of the SHG Quality Inspector or Leader.

### Stage 5: Packaging, Dispatch & Payout Release
- Accepted batches are packed according to moisture-proof shipping guidelines.
- Dispatch is coordinated with regional logistics carriers (e.g., KSRTC Cargo, India Post Parcel).
- Upon verified delivery receipt, individual artisan payouts are transferred directly to member accounts.
