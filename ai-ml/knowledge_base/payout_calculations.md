# Payout Calculations, Audit Rules, and Deduction Policies

## 1. Transparent Piece-Rate Compensation
Artisan members are compensated on a verified piece-rate basis for every unit passing quality inspection:
$$\text{Gross Earnings} = \text{Accepted Quantity} \times \text{Approved Unit Rate}$$
Units requiring minor rework are returned to the artisan with specific corrective instructions and re-inspected upon resubmission.

## 2. Permitted Deductions
Deductions from gross earnings are strictly limited to pre-agreed operational costs:
1. **Raw Material Advance:** If the SHG advanced high-value raw materials (e.g. pure silk, precious beads) purchased from common funds.
2. **Machinery Rental / Micro-finance Recovery:** Nominal monthly equipment installment agreed in group resolutions.

## 3. Financial Anomaly Audit Criteria
The system automatically flags potential irregularities for human review:
- **High Deduction Ratio:** If deductions exceed 40% of gross earnings, the transaction is flagged for mandatory supervisor justification.
- **Negative Payouts:** Any record yielding net payable amounts below ₹0 is immediately halted.
- **Arithmetic Inconsistencies:** Discrepancies between recorded net and $(\text{Gross} - \text{Deductions})$ exceeding ₹0.05 are flagged as calculation errors.
- **Over-production Claims:** Instances where accepted quantity exceeds initially assigned quantity require supervisor sign-off.
