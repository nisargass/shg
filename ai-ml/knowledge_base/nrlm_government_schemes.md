# Government Schemes and Funding for Women's SHGs

## 1. Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - NRLM (National Rural Livelihoods Mission)
Under the Ministry of Rural Development, DAY-NRLM provides intensive support to rural women's institutions.

### Key Financial Mechanisms
1. **Revolving Fund (RF):** A one-time catalytic grant of ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 provided to eligible SHGs practicing Panchasutra for at least 3 months, used to augment the group's internal lending pool.
2. **Community Investment Fund (CIF):** Substantial capital support provided through Village Organizations (VOs) to support livelihoods, enterprise expansion, and bulk procurement.
3. **Interest Subvention Scheme:** Concessional credit enabling eligible SHGs to access bank credit at an effective interest rate of 7% per annum (or 4% on prompt repayment in designated intensive districts).

## 2. Lakhpati Didi Initiative
The Government of India's Lakhpati Didi initiative aims to enable rural SHG women to achieve a sustainable annual household income of at least ₹1,00,000 through diversified micro-enterprises and market linkages.

## 3. Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises (PMFME)
Provides 35% credit-linked capital subsidy up to ₹10 Lakhs per enterprise for SHG members engaged in food processing (e.g. millet snacks, pickles, spices, honey).

## 4. Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)
Offers non-collateral micro-loans under three categories:
- **Shishu:** Loans up to ₹50,000 for startup tools and sewing machines.
- **Kishore:** Loans from ₹50,000 to ₹5 Lakhs for working capital and inventory bulk purchasing.
- **Tarun:** Loans from ₹5 Lakhs to ₹10 Lakhs for enterprise scaling and logistics equipment.
