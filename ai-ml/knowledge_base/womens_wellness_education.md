# Women's Occupational Health, Wellness, and Workplace Ergonomics

## 1. Occupational Ergonomics for Artisans and Tailors
Artisan work such as hand-embroidery, weaving, block printing, and machine tailoring involves repetitive movements and prolonged seated posture.

### Recommended Daily Workplace Habits
1. **The 30-Minute Posture Reset:** Every 30 minutes, pause work for 60 seconds to roll shoulders backwards, stretch fingers, and gently turn the neck from side to side.
2. **Proper Seating Posture:** Keep your back straight supported by a firm chair or cushion. Avoid hunching forward over stitching tables.
3. **Adequate Task Lighting:** Position lamps to avoid sharp glare and shadows on fabrics, reducing eye strain and headaches.
4. **Hydration & Ventilation:** Drink clean drinking water regularly (minimum 2 to 3 liters daily) and maintain proper cross-ventilation in shared production centers.

## 2. Nutrition and Anemia Awareness
- Rural and semi-urban women face high rates of nutritional anemia.
- A diet rich in iron (spinach, drumstick leaves/moringa, jaggery, lentils, dates) combined with Vitamin C (lemons, amla) significantly improves stamina and energy levels.

## 3. Important Medical Disclaimer
> **Medical Advisory:** This educational material provides general workplace ergonomics and health awareness for SHG members. Antigravity and SHG Saathi do not provide clinical medical diagnoses. If you experience persistent sharp pain, numbness in fingers, chest tightness, severe fatigue, or any abnormal symptoms, please consult a qualified healthcare professional at your nearest Primary Health Centre (PHC) or hospital immediately.
