# Product Pricing and Costing Guidelines for SHGs

## 1. Principles of Fair & Sustainable Pricing
SHGs frequently underprice their artisan goods by failing to account for indirect operational expenses and true artisan labor hours. A sustainable selling price must protect artisan livelihoods while remaining competitive in retail and corporate markets.

## 2. Standard Cost Elements
1. **Material Cost:** Fabric, threads, zippers, lining, dyes, packaging boxes, and labels.
2. **Artisan Labor Cost:** Fair piece-rate wages calculated based on hours required multiplied by statutory minimum artisan wage standards.
3. **Packaging Cost:** Primary polybags, silica gel packets, outer corrugated boxes, and branding stickers.
4. **Transportation & Logistics:** Local transit to courier hub, freight charges, and handling costs.
5. **Operational Expenses & Overhead Allowance:** Electricity for sewing machines, machine needle replacements, maintenance oil, and bank transaction charges.

## 3. Mathematical Formula for Selling Price
Profit margin should be expressed as a percentage of the **final selling price**, not merely as a markup over cost:
$$\text{Total Cost} = \text{Material} + \text{Labor} + \text{Packaging} + \text{Transport} + \text{Operational}$$
$$\text{Unit Cost} = \frac{\text{Total Cost}}{\text{Order Quantity}}$$
$$\text{Selling Price Per Unit} = \frac{\text{Unit Cost}}{1 - \frac{\text{Target Margin \%}}{100}}$$

Example: If unit cost is ₹150 and target margin is 15%:
$$\text{Selling Price} = \frac{150}{1 - 0.15} = \frac{150}{0.85} \approx ₹176.47$$

## 4. Omitted Cost Warnings
- **Machine Wear and Electricity:** Always budget at least ₹1.50 to ₹3.00 per unit for machine maintenance.
- **Packaging Buffer:** Jute and organic handloom products require moisture-resistant packaging during monsoon transit.
